Delete your account

Ghiraas app · Developer: Ghiraas (info@ghiras-app.com)

An account in Ghiraas is optional and is used only to move your data between your devices. If you never created one, nothing of yours is on our server; everything on your device is removed when you uninstall the app.

From inside the app

  1. Open Ghiraas, then Settings.
  2. Choose Account & sync.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Deletion happens immediately and cannot be undone.

Without the app

If you no longer have the app, email info@ghiras-app.com from the address you signed up with and say you want your account deleted. We delete it and its data, and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • The account itself: your email and account identifier.
  • Everything uploaded for sync: bookmarks, notes, reading position, khatmah and the rest.

All of it is deleted at once; we keep no copy.

What is not deleted

  • What is on your device stays as it is; uninstalling the app removes it.
  • The anonymous usage signal is tied to an install identifier, not to your account, and carries no name or email. Details are in the privacy policy.
  • Your messages to support are not part of the account. To have a conversation deleted, ask in it or by email.
