What's inside

It reminds you gently and keeps you company quietly. It doesn't keep score of your worship.

Adhkar and wirds

Morning, evening, sleep, after-prayer and more — each with its text, source and grading. A quiet counter and a focus mode.

The Mushaf and your wird

Mushaf pages, bookmarks and notes, your reading position kept, and a khatmah you move through at your own pace.

Prayer times

Astronomically computed times for your city, a reminder before each prayer, and adhkar reminders tied to prayer times, not a fixed clock.

Qibla

A compass pointing to the Qibla from where you are, with its bearing from north.

Hijri calendar

Today in Hijri and Gregorian, recommended fasting days, and a countdown to the next one.

Home-screen widgets

Prayer times, the dhikr of the moment and the Hijri date — without opening the app.

Inside the app

Four design directions, light and dark modes, and the text size you choose. The app's interface is in Arabic and English.

Your data is yours

Your counts, your reading and your city stay on your device, with no account needed. To move them between your devices, an account is optional and you can delete it at any time.

Offline

Adhkar, the Mushaf, prayer times and the Qibla work without a connection.

No ads

No ads in the app, no tracking.

Plainly stated

The little that is sent is described in detail in the privacy policy.

Support

A suggestion or a problem? Write to us from inside the app (Settings → Support & feedback), or by email.
