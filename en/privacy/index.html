Privacy policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Your dhikr, prayers and Quran reading stay on your device. We send an anonymous usage signal and the structure of the bundles you create. If you create an account for sync, what you choose to sync is uploaded to your account alone, and you can delete it at any time.

What changed. We added an optional account for moving your data between your devices, and describe what it uploads under “Account & sync”. If you do not create an account, nothing changes for you.

What we collect

1. An anonymous usage signal, when you open the app, at most once every half hour.

The daily “yes or no” is a single answer: we do not know how much you said, which dhikr, or when.

2. App crashes: nothing. If the app crashes, the trace is kept on your device only and is not sent to us. If that ever changes, we will update this page before releasing the version that changes it.

3. Bundles you create. When you create a custom bundle we send the name you gave it and what it contains: which library adhkar you chose and how many times each is repeated. This tells us which adhkar people choose, so we can improve the library.

4. Adhkar you write yourself. If you add a dhikr that is not in the app’s library, we send its text as you wrote it, with the repeat count you chose. We keep a copy to improve the library, and may add it there for everyone. Your dhikr works for you immediately either way.

The text of library adhkar is never sent; we already have it.

5. Custom Quran wirds. The surah and ayat you chose, the schedule (daily, Friday, or before sleep), and the name you gave it. This is what you defined, not what you have read.

6. Version check. When you open the app, Ghiraas asks our server for the oldest version still supported, so it can invite you to update if yours is too old. The request carries nothing about you, and without a connection the app opens as usual.

7. Your messages to us: optional, and only when you write. From “Support & feedback” in the app you can send a suggestion or report a problem. We receive:

  • The type (suggestion or problem) and the text you wrote;
  • Device information shown to you before sending: app version, operating system, device model, and the state of notifications and alarms;
  • Any photos or videos you choose to attach (up to five);
  • The install identifier, so our reply reaches you in the app.

Nothing here is sent unless you tap “Send”, and none of it is shared with anyone. We note when you read our reply, to know it reached you. To have a conversation deleted, ask in it.

Account & sync (optional)

The app works fully without an account. If you create one from Settings → Account & sync:

  • Your email address: for signing in only. We send it a one-time sign-in code, never other mail, and there is no password.
  • What syncs: your mushaf bookmarks and their categories, your notes, your reading position, and your khatmah and reading history. If we add anything else, we will list it here first.
  • This data belongs to your account alone: no other user can read it, and we do not use it for statistics or for anything other than moving it between your devices.
  • Deleting it: Settings → Account & sync → Delete account removes the account and everything uploaded to it immediately. See Delete your account.

The install identifier

An anonymous code derived from your device in a way that cannot be reversed: it does not reveal who you are or which device you use, and cannot be linked to any other app.

It is stable on purpose: it survives uninstalling and reinstalling, so a reinstall is not counted as a new user. A factory reset changes it. It is not linked to your account if you create one.

What we never collect

The text of any library dhikr, your counters, your city or location, your name, phone number or contacts, or your advertising ID. We do not collect your email or your reading unless you create an account for sync, as described above. There are no ads in the app and no social-media tracking.

Is the signal optional?

The usage signal is part of the app and cannot be turned off, which is why we keep it to the minimum: no counters, no dhikr text, no identity, no location. The account and messages to us are optional.

Where your data is kept

Dhikr logs, bundles, settings, Quran progress, achievements and your prayer city are kept on your device, inside the app.

What reaches us (the signal, messages, and account data if you create one) is kept with a cloud hosting provider that processes it on our behalf only, not for its own purposes. Sign-in codes are sent through an email provider. We do not sell your data or share it with advertisers or analytics companies.

Permissions

  • Notifications: for prayer and dhikr reminders, controlled in settings.
  • Exact alarms: so a reminder lands at the prayer time itself, not near it.
  • Approximate location (optional): prayer times come from a city you pick from a list, which needs no permission. If you tap “Use my location”, Ghiraas reads an approximate location once and saves it on your device in place of the city. It is not sent anywhere and is never read in the background.
  • Do Not Disturb (Android, optional): only if you turn on “Silent after the adhan”. Ghiraas turns Do Not Disturb on after the prayer for the time you chose, then off again, and sees nothing of your calls or messages. You can revoke it in your device settings at any time.
  • Vibration: haptic feedback when you tap the counter.

Children

Ghiraas is suitable for all ages and needs no personal information. Nothing that identifies anyone reaches us unless they choose to give it: an email for an optional account, or a message to us.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update this page and its date, and point it out in the app.

Contact

Questions: email info@ghiras-app.com, or write from the app (Settings → Support & feedback).
